We want you to feel safe and secure when connecting with businesses. If you feel that a business you’re connecting with isn’t following our policies, you can report the account to WhatsApp.
To report a business
Tap the chat with the business, then tap the business name to see the business profile.
Scroll to the bottom and tap Report Business > Report.
If you’d also like to block the business, tap Report and Block. Select your reason under Reason for blocking, then tap Block.
Note: WhatsApp receives up to five of the last messages sent to you by the reported user or group and, if a call took place, the basic info about the last five calls with them, such as who initiated the call and the duration of the call. The user or group won’t be notified. WhatsApp also receives the reported group or user ID, information on when the message was sent, and the type of message sent (image, video, text, etc.).
