Every end-user customer can raise a complaint with respect to a United Payments Interface (UPI) transaction, on the PSP app/TPAP app.
The end-user customer can select the relevant transaction and raise a complaint in relation thereto.
A complaint must first be raised with the relevant TPAP for any UPI-related issues. This applies to end-user customers who were onboarded by the PSP Bank (if the UPI transaction is made through TPAP app). In case the complaint/grievance remains unresolved, the next level for escalation will be the PSP Bank. Unresolved issues are escalated to the customer's bank (where the end-user customer maintains its account) and National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI), in the same order. After exercising these options, the end-user customer can approach the Banking Ombudsman and/or the Ombudsman for Digital Complaints, as the case may be.
The complaint can be raised for both the types of transactions i.e. fund transfer and merchant transactions.
The PSP or TPAP shall keep the end-user customer informed by updating the complaint status on the relevant app
