Add your first payment method for ads
You add your first payment method when you create your first ad. Until then, you won't see payment options in your ad account settings.
Tap Tools > Advertise, then create your ad and tap Create ad.
Enter your email address > tap Send code, then enter the six-digit code.
Select your country or region, currency, and time zone > tap Next.
Add a payment method > tap Next.
Enter your payment information > tap Save, then enter your business information > tap Save.
You don't need a credit or debit card to pay for ads. After your first ad, you can add more payment methods from your ad account settings.
Supported payment methods
The payments feature on the WhatsApp Business app is enabled by Meta Pay. Payments are processed by supported payment partners.
You can pay for Click to WhatsApp ads with one of the following accepted payment methods:
Credit or debit cards (American Express, Discover, Mastercard, Visa, JCB)
PayPal
Bank account (direct debit) in supported countries
Local manual payment methods in supported countries and currencies
The payment methods available to you vary, depending on the country and currency of your ad account. Learn more about supported payments partners.
Change your payment method
To change your payment method, tap Change and select from the available payment options.
For catalogs in the WhatsApp Business app, the payment currency is auto-selected based on the country code of your registered phone number and can't be updated.
