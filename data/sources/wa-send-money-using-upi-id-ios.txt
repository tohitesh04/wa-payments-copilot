Tap Settings or your profile picture, then tap Payments > Send Payment.
Select Enter a UPI ID or UPI number then enter the UPI ID or UPI number.
Tap Next.
Enter the amount you want to send > tap Next.
Tap Send Payment.
Enter a UPI ID to confirm your payment.
Note:
While your personal messages on WhatsApp are always end-to-end encrypted, the payment descriptions you add to your transactions aren’t.
To use payments, make sure you're using the latest version of WhatsApp. If you're having trouble downloading it through the Google Play Store, visit whatsapp.com/download.
