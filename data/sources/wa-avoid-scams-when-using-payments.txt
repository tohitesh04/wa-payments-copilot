WhatsApp will never reach out to you asking for payment or bank information. If we reach out for another reason, it’ll be via a legitimate channel—usually a chat from an official WhatsApp account that has a verified badge Meta Verified Badge (sticker with a checkmark). You can always contact us to double check if a message, email, or phone call came from us.
Don’t click on any suspicious links, or links from unknown users.
Remember that approving a payment request will withdraw funds from your account. Only approve payment requests from trusted contacts.
If your phone is lost or stolen, inform your bank and contact us by visiting whatsapp.com and selecting Contact us.
If you receive a message from an unknown number, and you think it might be a scam, you can block the number and report it. To do so:
Android: Tap Block, then select Report to WhatsApp. Tap Block.
iOS: Tap Block > Block and report.
Web: Click Block, then select Report to WhatsApp. Click Block.
Mac: Click Block > Block and report.
Windows: Click Block then select Report to WhatsApp. Click Block.
